FOUNDERS

Welcome Message from the Founders

Dear Friends, Members, Partners, and Supporters,

Welcome to Voice of Unity Ministry International.

It is with great joy and gratitude to God that we welcome you to the vision and mission of this ministry. Voice of Unity Ministry International was founded in response to God's calling to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, bring healing to broken lives, disciple believers, and unite people from every background in the love of Christ.

Our passion is to see lives transformed through the power of God's Word, communities restored through practical acts of compassion, and nations impacted by the message of hope and salvation. Together, let us be a united voice that proclaims the love of Christ, restores hope, and transforms lives for the glory of God.

May the Lord richly bless you as you partner with us in advancing His Kingdom.

In Christ's Service,

EVANGELISM

Evangelism

Evangelism

Voice of unity ministries international is rooted deeply in the Great Commission, as Jesus commands in Matthew 28:19–20: “Go therefore and make disciples of all nations… teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you.” Therefore we want to make disciples who make disciples. We do this command through Discovery Bible Studies, evangelism outreaches, leadership trainings, and ongoing discipleship.

Today, God is at work through many trained leaders and spread across the country of Uganda, and in so doing many people are joining and increasing in the faith and changing communities. We have seen numerous people come to faith in our lord Jesus Christ.

Where We Serve

Our head office is located in Gayaza, Kasangati Town Council, in Wakiso District but Voice of unity ministries international currently serves communities in various districts across Uganda, with a strong focus on the regions where many of people have never heard the Gospel.

Outreach Impact

Through our gospel outreaches we have managed to bring peace among many ministries that we not at good terms with their neighbors in various communities since we encourage them to work together with us as we proclaim the good news of the kingdom of our God jointly and so encouraging reconciliation.so raising and developing new leaders with a Christ like mentality by deligently teaching the word of God.

We have also managed to do film evangelism, and helped plant and strengthen churches.

Sharing the Gospel Across Uganda

Voice of unity ministries international has had an incredible privilege of sharing the Gospel with thousands of people across Ugandaover the past 3 years. By God’s grace, many of these individuals chose to follow Jesus, committing their lives to Him and beginning their journey as new disciples. Among them were people from a variety of backgrounds, including individuals from unreached communities and Muslim families, showing the life changing power of the Gospel in many corners of the country like Rukwingiri, Mbarara, Isingiro,Wakiso,

Karamoja, Kayunga districts etc. We thank God in these life-changing decisions and give all glory to God for the fruit of His Kingdom.

Annual Evangelism Outreaches

Each year, volunteers from Kampala travel to different parts of Uganda for evangelism outreaches. Together, we share the Gospel with thousands, witness many salvations, and continue expanding into new and unreached areas year after year.

STATEMENT OF FAITH

Statement of Faith

Voice of Unity Ministry International is founded upon the Holy Scriptures and is committed to proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ in accordance with biblical truth. We believe that the Holy Bible is the inspired, authoritative, and infallible Word of God, revealing His will and serving as the final authority in all matters of faith and Christian living.

We believe in one eternal God, existing in three Persons—the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. We believe that Jesus Christ is the divine Son of God, the only Savior of humanity, and the only way to the Father through His crucifixion, death, burial, resurrection, ascension, and eternal Lordship.

We believe that salvation is offered by God's grace to all who repent of their sins and place their faith in Jesus Christ. We affirm the ministry and power of the Holy Spirit, who regenerates believers, equips them for service, and continues to work through spiritual gifts, healing, miracles, signs, and wonders according to God's will.

We believe in water baptism by immersion, the fellowship of believers for worship, prayer, teaching, and discipleship, and the Church as the Body of Christ, into which all believers are born through the regenerating work of the Holy Spirit.

We believe that all people are sinners in need of redemption, that Satan and demonic powers are real but subject to the authority of Jesus Christ, and that the Gospel is God's appointed means of redeeming humanity and establishing His eternal Kingdom.

We believe in the resurrection of the dead, the gift of eternal life for those who belong to Christ, and eternal judgment for those who reject Him. We further affirm holy and righteous living, acts of charity and compassion, the Great Commission to proclaim the Gospel to all people without discrimination, the biblical training of believers under the guidance of the Holy Spirit, the ordination of women into ministry, and the observance of Sunday as the historic day of Christian worship in remembrance of the resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ.

These biblical convictions guide the doctrine, ministry, leadership, worship, evangelism, and mission of Voice of Unity Ministry International as we seek to glorify God and make disciples of all nations.

COMMITMENT TO TRANSPARENCY

Commitment to Transparency

At Voice of Unity Ministry International, we are committed to conducting all our activities with the highest standards of integrity, accountability, and transparency. We believe that faithful stewardship is a biblical responsibility and an essential foundation for building trust with our members, partners, donors, beneficiaries, and the wider community.

To uphold this commitment, we pledge to:

Manage all financial resources responsibly and in accordance with sound accounting principles.
Maintain accurate and complete records of all ministry activities and finances.
Provide regular financial and ministry reports to the appropriate leadership, partners, and stakeholders.
Ensure that donations and other resources are used solely for their intended purposes and in support of the ministry's mission.
Promote ethical leadership, honesty, and accountability at every level of the ministry.
Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and governance requirements.
Foster an environment where questions, feedback, and constructive recommendations are welcomed and addressed respectfully.
Continually review and strengthen our governance and financial management practices to ensure excellence in stewardship.

As servants of God, we recognize that we are accountable not only to those who support this ministry but ultimately to God. Therefore, we are committed to operating with openness, faithfulness, and integrity in every aspect of our work, bringing glory to God while faithfully serving His people.

RESPONSIBLE STEWARDSHIP OF RESOURCES

Responsible Stewardship of Resources

At Voice of Unity Ministry International, we recognize that every resource entrusted to us—whether financial, human, material, or spiritual—is a gift from God and must be managed with wisdom, integrity, and accountability. We are committed to being faithful stewards by ensuring that all resources are used efficiently, transparently, and purposefully to advance the Kingdom of God and fulfill the ministry's mission.

To demonstrate this commitment, we shall:

Use all financial resources prudently and only for approved ministry purposes.
Maintain accurate financial records and implement sound accounting and internal control systems.
Prepare and adhere to responsible budgets while ensuring proper authorization for expenditures.
Safeguard ministry assets, equipment, and property through proper management and maintenance.
Encourage generosity and responsible giving while honoring the trust of donors, partners, and supporters.
Value and develop the gifts, talents, and time of staff, volunteers, and ministry workers as vital resources for God's work.
Promote efficiency, avoid waste, and seek cost-effective solutions without compromising the quality of ministry services.
Conduct regular monitoring, evaluation, and reporting to ensure that resources are achieving their intended impact.
Comply with all applicable legal, financial, and governance requirements.
Uphold biblical principles of honesty, faithfulness, and accountability in every aspect of resource management.

We affirm that faithful stewardship is both a spiritual responsibility and a practical obligation.

As accountable servants of God, we are committed to managing every resource entrusted to Voice of Unity Ministry International with excellence, integrity, and transparency for the glory of God and the benefit of the communities we serve.
